TAS+ Workflow Overview

The TAS+ platform orchestrates every step of the transport appointment scheduling process, empowering shippers, drivers, and gate teams with the information they need to keep cargo moving without delays.

1 Request Submitted

Shipper, importer, or factory submits a pickup or drop-off request via TAS+.

2 Auto-Dispatch

Smart matching engine assigns the best-fit truck and driver from your fleet partners.

3 API Booking

TAS API instantly reserves a slot with live gate times and sends confirmations.

4 Driver Mobile App

Drivers receive load details, optimized routes, and time windows directly on-device.

5 Gate & Yard Handling

Gate teams process entries, manage containers, and sync yard status automatically.

6 Digital Proof

Handover is confirmed and digital proof of delivery is sent to every stakeholder.
